Privacy statement RittenRegistratie-bot

Version 3, 2 November 2026.

This is a translation of the Dutch Privacyverklaring. If the two differ, the Dutch version prevails.

Du7 Technology Services ($company_line_en) is the controller (verwerkingsverantwoordelijke) for the personal data that RittenRegistratie-bot processes. Questions or requests: $support_email.

What data

  • Sign-up: name, email address, WhatsApp number, the name of your car, starting odometer reading and starting address, and, if you fill them in, your company name and billing address.
  • Trips: what you send to the bot: odometer readings, destinations and addresses, private or business, notes, and a shared location if you send one. A journey can reveal something about where you live or work, so we treat trips as confidential.
  • Settings for your number: the language you choose (Dutch or English) and the time of your last message to the bot. We use that time only to know whether WhatsApp will still let an ordinary message through or whether we have to send an approved template message.
  • Cancellation code: if you cancel through the website, we send a 6-digit code via WhatsApp. We keep only an encrypted form of it, for at most 10 minutes, and not on disk.
  • Payment: your Mollie customer ID, and the status of payments and mandates. Only Mollie sees your full bank account or card details.
  • Invoices: after each paid period, an invoice with your name, your company name and billing address if given, the amount, the VAT (btw) and the period. We send it via WhatsApp.
  • Technical: the web server logs IP address, time and requested page, so that we can investigate outages and misuse. The content of your WhatsApp messages does not end up in those logs.

What for, and on what legal basis

  • Keeping and delivering your trip log, billing the subscription, making cancellation possible and informing you about the service: performance of the contract.
  • Keeping payment details and invoices: legal obligation (the statutory tax record-keeping requirement).
  • Logs and backups for security, troubleshooting and recovery: legitimate interest.

We do not sell anything on, do not use your data for advertising, do not build profiles and do not make automated decisions about you.

Who else sees it

These parties process data for the service, each only for their own part:

  • WhatsApp Ireland Ltd. (Meta) carries the messages, codes and files between you and the bot, on our behalf. WhatsApp Business terms
  • Mollie B.V. (Amsterdam) processes the payments and mandates. Mollie is itself responsible for this, under its own privacy statement. Mollie's privacy statement
  • Google Ireland Ltd. (Google Maps) converts a shared location into an address, only if you share a location. Google's privacy policy
  • Cloudflare, Inc. manages the DNS for rrbot.nl and forwards mail sent to $support_email. Cloudflare's privacy policy

Meta, Google and Cloudflare may process data outside the EU. They do so on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your trips and sign-up details are stored on our own server in the Netherlands, with a daily backup to a separate disk on our premises. Each customer has their own trip log and their own address book; other customers see nothing of yours.

How long

  • Trips, addresses, your language choice and the time of your last message: for as long as your subscription runs, and then $retention_days more days so that you can still request your overview. After that we delete them.
  • Backups: 30 days. After that, deleted data is gone from the backups as well.
  • Sign-up and payment details and invoices: seven years after the end of the subscription (statutory tax record-keeping requirement).
  • Server logs: at most 30 days.

Cookies and statistics

rrbot.nl sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers or analytics. We only count, ourselves, per day, how often each page was viewed, which website visitors came from (only the domain name, such as google.com), which campaign code was in the link and how many sign-up forms were submitted. These counts contain no IP addresses, no cookies and nothing that could identify a visitor. Mollie's payment pages fall under Mollie's cookie policy.

Security

The site is only reachable over HTTPS, the server sits behind a firewall and only we have access to it. A payment confirmation from Mollie is always checked with Mollie itself. Cancelling through the website is only possible with a code sent to the subscription's WhatsApp number.

Your rights

You may access your data, have it corrected or deleted, have its processing restricted, object, and take your data with you (send excel all, or email us for a complete copy). We respond within one month. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (the Dutch Data Protection Authority).

Changes

If we change this statement materially, we will let you know via WhatsApp or email.
