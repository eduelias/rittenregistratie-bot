Who it's for

Mainly for employees with a company or lease car who want to show they drive no more than 500 km privately a year. That record backs up your Verklaring geen privégebruik auto, the statement that saves you the bijtelling (the taxable benefit for private use). Also for self-employed people (zzp'ers) and business owners with a car or van of the business, and for anyone who drives for work in their own car and wants to track their business kilometres. If you keep a trip log in a Dutch spreadsheet now, you can send one WhatsApp message per trip instead.

Not sure what the rules are? Read what a Dutch trip log must contain, in English, with a source for every rule.

What you get

Every required field

Per trip: date, start and end reading, departure and arrival address, private or business, and the route when it's not your usual one.

English chat, Dutch paperwork

The bot talks to you in English. The Excel uses the Dutch column names (Datum, Beginstand, Eindstand, ...) that your employer and the Belastingdienst expect.

Nothing changed afterwards

Every trip is recorded once, as you sent it. Corrections are added as a new line; the Excel is always rebuilt from that record.

Private kilometres in view

The bot counts your private kilometres per year and warns you as you get close to 500 km.

RittenRegistratie-bot records what you send it. It isn't tax advice and doesn't guarantee that the Belastingdienst accepts your records; you stay responsible for them being correct.

Questions

Why do I pay € $mandate_en first?

That payment, by iDEAL or credit card, gives the mandate that lets the subscription be charged automatically after the trial. Your bank shows it as a payment to Du7 Technology Services via Mollie.

Is the Excel in English?

The column names are the Dutch ones, because the Belastingdienst and Dutch employers work with those terms. Your addresses and notes stay exactly as you sent them. The bot's messages, help and menus are in English.

Can I use a foreign phone number?

Yes, any number that has WhatsApp. Enter it with the country code, for example +44 7700 900123.

How do I cancel?

Send unsubscribe to the bot, use the cancel page (linked at the bottom of every page), or email us. Nothing is charged after that, and the bot keeps working until the end of your paid period (or trial). Send excel all before then to keep all your trips.

Do I need an app or a device?

No. Just WhatsApp on the number you sign up with.

What happens to my data?

Your trips are stored on our own server in the Netherlands and only used to make your trip log. Messages go through WhatsApp (Meta), payments through Mollie. See the privacy statement.

I have more cars or several drivers.

The bot can do that, but it isn't in the online signup yet. Email us and we'll set it up.
