Privacy Policy

Service: RittenRegistratie-Bot — a self-hosted WhatsApp bot for recording personal/business car trips (rittenregistratie).

Operator: Du7 Technology Services

Last updated: 25 July 2026

1. Overview

RittenRegistratie-Bot is a private, self-hosted tool. It receives WhatsApp messages that you send to it and records the trip information they contain (odometer readings, destinations) into a spreadsheet stored on the operator's own server. It is intended for the operator's own use and explicitly authorised users only.

2. Information we process

  • WhatsApp message content you send to the bot (e.g. an odometer reading and a destination), and, if you choose to share it, a location you send.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number, used solely to identify which vehicle a message belongs to and to reply to you.
  • Derived trip records (date, start/end odometer, start/end address, distance, business/private classification).

We do not collect message content from chats other than those sent directly to the bot. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

3. How we use the information

  • To generate and maintain a trip log (rittenregistratie) for tax record-keeping purposes.
  • To reply to you with a confirmation of the recorded trip.
  • Optionally, to resolve an address from a place name or a shared location via the Google Maps API.

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on the legitimate interest of, and consent given by, the authorised users who choose to send trips to the bot, and on the need to keep accurate mileage administration.

5. Data sharing

Trip data is stored locally on the operator's self-hosted server and is not sold or shared with third parties, except:

  • Meta/WhatsApp, which transmits the messages between you and the bot (subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy).
  • Google Maps Platform, only when address/route lookup is enabled, and only the addresses/coordinates needed for that lookup.

6. Data retention

Trip records are retained for as long as required for tax administration (typically up to 7 years under Dutch law) or until the operator deletes them. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Security

Incoming webhooks are verified cryptographically, only allow-listed phone numbers are accepted, and the service is exposed over HTTPS. Access tokens and secrets are not stored in the public source code.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to its processing, by contacting the operator.

9. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the operator via the project repository: github.com/eduelias/rittenregistratie-bot.
